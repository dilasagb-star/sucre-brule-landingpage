Our story

Sugar, burnt just right.

Every autumn begins with the same small ritual: a candle lit early, a book open on the table, something sweet in the air. Sucre Brûlé was born from that moment.

The name comes from the crack of caramelised sugar, the second where sweetness turns warm and golden. We wanted to capture that feeling in a fragrance: soft, glowing and a little bit luxurious.

Sucre Brûlé is for autumn lovers who adore warm, sweet scents and the quiet confidence of a niche fragrance.

  • Warmlike candlelight
  • Sweetlike caramel
  • Cosylike home

The fragrance experience

Three moments, one warm glow.

Imagine the first spray on a cold October evening. Sucre Brûlé unfolds slowly, in three moments.

  1. 01

    Crackle

    The first spray opens with caramelised sugar: sweet, bright and a little bit playful, like the top of a crème brûlée.

  2. 02

    Glow

    Then the warmth arrives. Golden amber and soft vanilla wrap around the sweetness and turn it into something deep and comforting.

  3. 03

    Linger

    Hours later, a soft, cosy trail stays behind on your scarf and your skin, like the memory of a warm room.
